What Is ARDS?

Regulatory compliance generates mountains of data — emission measurements, obligation records, audit trails, verdicts. Today that data is locked in proprietary formats, siloed by vendor, and incomprehensible across borders. ARDS fixes that.

A Common Schema Language

ARDS defines canonical JSON schemas for every artefact in the regulatory compliance lifecycle. If it has an ARDS schema, it can be read by any ARDS-conformant tool — today and in ten years.

A Conformance Framework

Six progressive conformance levels let implementors signal exactly how ARDS-compatible their tools are — from basic schema validity all the way to full platform certification.

An Open Governance Model

ARDS is governed by the AxiomOrdo Standards Board with open working groups, a published RFC process, and a user council. Anyone can propose. AxiomOrdo chairs.

Six Normative Artefact Types

ARDS defines the complete lifecycle of a compliance record, from raw obligation through to audited package.

ROR Regulatory Obligation Record

Captures a single regulatory obligation — the rule, the target entity, the applicable period, and the applicable domain pack. The anchor for everything that follows.

EA Evidence Artefact

A structured record of evidence supporting compliance — measurement data, certificates, declarations, lab reports. Carries provenance and verification status.

CV Compliance Verdict

The formal outcome: compliant, non-compliant, or under-review. Links directly to the obligation record and the evidence used to reach the verdict.

TB Trace Bundle

A machine-readable graph of provenance links connecting obligations, evidence, and verdicts. Enables automated audit trails and regulator-ready exports.

UCA Unified Compliance Artefact

A composite envelope containing obligation, evidence, and verdict in a single portable document. The standard submission format for cross-border regulatory filings.

AP Audit Package

A sealed, signed collection of all artefacts for a given entity and period. Designed for regulator submission and long-term archival with cryptographic integrity.

Conformance Levels

Six tiers, each building on the last. Implementors self-declare or seek AxiomOrdo certification for levels 3–6.

Level Name Key Requirement Certification
L1 Schema-Valid Passes JSON Schema validation against the declared ARDS schema Self-declare
L2 ARDS Core Conformant Implements full ards:meta envelope with all required fields Self-declare
L3 ARDS Extended Conformant Implements trace links, provenance chain, and domain-specific extensions AxiomOrdo audited
L4 ARDS Certified Generator Tool produces ARDS artefacts at L3; passes full test suite AxiomOrdo certified
L5 ARDS Certified Consumer Tool ingests and processes ARDS artefacts from any certified generator AxiomOrdo certified
L6 ARDS Certified Platform End-to-end platform: generates, stores, traces, and exports ARDS artefacts AxiomOrdo certified

Why ARDS Wins

Regulators demand interoperability. Enterprises demand auditability. Vendors demand portability. ARDS is the only standard built for all three.

Regulator-Ready

ARDS Audit Packages carry cryptographic signatures, full provenance chains, and machine-readable metadata designed for automated regulatory ingestion.

Vendor-Neutral

AOSL-1.0 licence means any vendor can implement ARDS for free. The standard is openly governed. No lock-in. No licence fees. No permission required to build.

Future-Proof

Semantic versioning and a published RFC process ensure ARDS evolves without breaking existing implementations. Your data stays readable for as long as the regulation lasts.

Advisory panel · Nine founding positions

ARDS Maritime Founding Panel

The panel is being established to provide senior maritime operational scrutiny during the formative development of ARDS. The ARDS Maritime Founding Panel will examine how the AxiomOrdo Regulatory Data Standard represents, preserves and connects regulatory obligations, evidence, provenance, authority, human oversight, compliance conclusions and consequential decision records in maritime use.

Advisory status: panel members provide scrutiny and feedback. Ownership and publication authority remain with AxiomOrdo and the formally designated ARDS governance structure.

The founding mandate

  • Test the operational practicality of ARDS requirements.
  • Assess whether required evidence and records would withstand audit or investigation.
  • Challenge the clarity of accountability and human oversight.
  • Check alignment with maritime management-system realities.
  • Review terminology, controls and conformance requirements for consistent application.
  • Identify unnecessary burden, ambiguity or unintended risk.
  • Shape maritime-specific requirements for the ARDS Maritime Domain Pack.

Who the panel is for

  • Marine QHSE Managers
  • Designated Persons Ashore
  • Heads of Marine Assurance
  • Senior maritime auditors
  • Marine compliance leaders
  • Equivalent professionals with significant management-system or regulatory accountability

The panel is not a general-interest discussion group. It is intended for professionals whose judgement is already relied upon in consequential maritime decisions.

Nine founding positions

  • Each member must be able to make a substantive contribution.
  • Discussion must remain technically focused.
  • Individual comments must be properly considered and recorded.
  • Founding recognition must remain meaningful.

Appointment will be by invitation or direct selection. Pilot participation, commercial engagement or an expression of interest does not guarantee appointment.

Selection considerations

  • Seniority and relevance of responsibilities
  • Maritime QHSE, DPA, audit, assurance or compliance experience
  • Understanding of management systems and regulatory accountability
  • Experience reviewing evidence, incidents, risks or organisational controls
  • Ability to challenge technical material constructively
  • Independence of professional judgement
  • Willingness to participate personally
  • Capacity during the founding period
  • Contribution to the collective range of maritime experience

Founding recognition

Members may use the title ARDS Maritime Founding Panel Member. Subject to member consent, acknowledgement may appear in:

  • The ARDS founding record
  • Development and consultation reports
  • The ARDS website and published materials
  • The initial Maritime Domain Pack
  • Approved professional announcements and panel communications

Professional standing

  • Appointment is intended to be a selective professional distinction.
  • Members can influence ARDS while its maritime application is being established.
  • Documented scrutiny will contribute to the provenance of the standard's development.
  • The role carries recognition and responsibility.

Participation commitment

Approximately two hours per week during the initial ARDS development period.

  • Review materials
  • Attend panel discussions
  • Provide concise written feedback
  • Participate personally and consistently

Participation conditions

  • Terms of reference
  • Confidentiality and intellectual-property conditions
  • Conflict-of-interest disclosure and recusal arrangements
  • Minimum participation expectations
  • Rules for public references to ARDS and membership

What membership does not provide

  • Automatic or preferential ARDS certification
  • Reduced future assessment requirements
  • Control or ownership of ARDS intellectual property
  • Guaranteed commercial work
  • Authority to approve matters on behalf of ARDS
  • Preferential treatment for the member's employer
  • Regulatory endorsement or accreditation

Relationship to the Founding Operator Pilot

The Meriden pilot tests the practical value and usability of the Maritime AI Readiness Framework. The ARDS panel scrutinises the emerging standard.

Strong pilot contribution may identify suitable candidates, but appointment remains separate, selective and based on the needs of the ARDS founding programme.

Founding invitation

A limited number of senior Marine QHSE Managers, DPAs and equivalent maritime assurance leaders may be invited to join the nine-member ARDS Maritime Founding Panel. Appointment will recognise individuals whose experience and judgement can materially strengthen the credibility and operational relevance of ARDS.

Nine founding positions. Selected for professional standing, operational judgement and the ability to influence ARDS at the point of formation.
